\ann{Lock on\ldots{}} & Then click the map: the belly camera stays on that point of the ground. \\
\ann{Nearest object} & Locks the belly camera on the nearest aircraft, ship or vehicle FlightGear has. \\
\ann{Unlock} & Frees the camera: it stays where it is turned. \\
\ann{Level horizon} & Keeps the horizon level in the camera's picture as the aircraft banks. \\
\ann{$\blacktriangleleft$} & Turns the camera left, or moves a locked point left, by about a tenth of the picture. \\
\ann{$\blacktriangle$} & The same, up. \\
\ann{$\blacktriangledown$} & The same, down. \\
\ann{$\blacktriangleright$} & The same, right. \\
