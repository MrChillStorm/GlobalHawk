\textbf{Control} & \textbf{What it does} \\
\midrule
\ann{Hand over to} & The station to offer control to: one the aircraft is hearing. \\
\ann{Offer control} & Offers control to that station, once the three checks above it are ticked. \\
\ann{Withdraw the offer} & Takes the offer back; an offer lapses by itself after two minutes. \\
\ann{Accept control} & At the station offered to: takes control, once its own three checks are ticked. \\
\ann{Take control} & Shown only when the station that had control is no longer heard and the link is declared lost. \\
\bottomrule
