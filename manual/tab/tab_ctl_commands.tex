\toprule
\textbf{Control} & \textbf{What it does} \\
\midrule
\ann{Demo plan} & Loads the plan that ships with the aircraft, for the made-up field of the simulator server and the demo. \\
\ann{Plan from here} & Lays a local flight from the runway the aircraft stands on: take-off straight ahead, a box at 15,000 ft, a landing on the same runway. \\
\ann{Plan file} & Loads a plan from a file. \\
\ann{Edit plan} & Opens the editor on the plan the aircraft holds, or on the one just loaded. \\
\ann{Start} & Begins the plan: taxi and take-off. \\
\ann{Go to\ldots{}} & Then click the map: the aircraft leaves its plan, flies to the point at the height given and holds over it. \\
\ann{Hold here} & The aircraft circles to the right from where it is. \\
\ann{Altitude\ldots{}} & Changes the height of the leg or hold being flown. \\
\ann{Resume plan} & Back to the leg that was left. \\
\ann{Return home} & Flies the lost-link route and lands. \\
\ann{Link time-out\ldots{}} & Sets how long the aircraft may go without hearing the station in control before it leaves its plan for the lost-link route. Only the station in control; the aircraft takes 10 to 300 s. \\
