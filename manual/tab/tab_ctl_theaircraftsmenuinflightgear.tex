\textbf{Item} & \textbf{What it does} \\
\midrule
\ann{Select livery...} & Opens FlightGear's livery selector: White (the default), Finnish Air Force (GH-601) and Dark grey (military). The choice paints the aircraft at once and is kept for the next start. \\
\ann{Autostart} & Starts the engine: the rotors run up in about half a minute. \\
\ann{Shutdown} & Stops the engine. A plan's start is refused with the engine off. \\
\ann{Belly camera: lock and slew...} & Opens the camera's dialog. \\
\ann{Belly camera: lock on the cross} & Locks the camera on the point of the ground under its cross. \\
\ann{Belly camera: lock on the nearest object} & Locks it on the nearest aircraft, ship or vehicle. \\
\ann{Belly camera: level horizon on/off} & Keeps the horizon level in the camera's picture, or lets it lean with the aircraft. \\
\ann{Belly camera: unlock} & Frees the camera. \\
\ann{Ground to the horizon at height on/off} & Lets the terrain range grow with the height above the ground (on at the start), or leaves FlightGear's own setting. \\
